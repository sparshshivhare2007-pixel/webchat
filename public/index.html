About RegionalChat

RegionalChat is a platform to connect people from the same region. Chat, share files, and make voice/video calls with people in your area.

Features:

  • 🎙️ Voice & Video calls
  • 📎 File sharing
  • 👥 Group chats
  • 🌙 Dark mode
